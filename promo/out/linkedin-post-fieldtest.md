## Post copy (EN)

92 hydrocarbon releases were reported on the UK continental shelf in 2024. Every one of them started as a measurement someone took in the field.

And that is still where integrity data dies: on paper, in a spreadsheet, or behind the last bar of signal.

We rebuilt the workflow for the place where the work happens — the tablet in the field.

IntegrityWatch Field is an offline-first integrity and compliance centre:

→ Register the asset from real line data: diameter, wall thickness, MAWP, fluid
→ Import a field CSV — the app maps the columns, validates every row, commits on device (32 rows, 0 skipped in our run)
→ Risk band, inspection interval and PDF dossier generated offline
→ Corrosion, PSV sizing (API 520/526), pipelines, API 580, rules R1–R17, 7 languages, light/dark

No server. No cloud. No connection.

The test run behind this video used 55 real UKCS pipeline records from NSTA open data — not dummy rows. Asset attributes are real; inspection series are derived and labelled as such.
